Minecraft Education — HTU Digital Campus

A digital twin of a university, built so students could walk through their future.

Minecraft EducationRedstone & Command BlocksEducational Design

As part of a collaboration with Minecraft Education, we worked on creating educational experiences designed for students and children. The project that stood out to me most was the creation of a digital twin of our university inside Minecraft Education.

The goal was to give prospective students an interactive way to explore the university, learn about its unique academic system, visit different spaces, and interact with instructors through the Minecraft environment. This was particularly valuable because the university follows a different educational model from many other universities in Jordan and has introduced Game Development as a new career path.

My Role

My main responsibility was designing and building the game space within Minecraft. I created interactive NPCs using Redstone and command blocks, as well as a simple trivia game to make the experience more engaging and educational.

I am particularly proud of this project because I was responsible for building the part that introduced and showcased the university’s new Game Development pathway. It combined educational design, game mechanics, and interactive world-building into an experience that could help students discover a university and a potential career through a medium they already enjoy.
