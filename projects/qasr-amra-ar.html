Qasr Amra — AR Heritage Experience

Heritage Hackathon project — 2nd place, national level.

UnityAugmented RealityHackathon

Qasr Amra is an augmented reality experience designed to make Jordan’s historical sites more engaging and meaningful to local visitors. The project addresses a common issue in local tourism: many people visit heritage sites without fully understanding their historical and cultural significance, making it harder to develop a genuine appreciation for them.

Our concept transforms a visit to Qasr Amra into an interactive AR experience. Visitors can use their device to explore the site while receiving real-time information about its architecture, symbols, and historical elements. AR overlays can explain the purpose of different structures, reveal how spaces were used, introduce the people who once lived there, and provide context for the artwork and icons found throughout the site.

My Role — Unity & Technical Development

I was responsible for the technical side of the project, including the Unity development and the creation of the project video. I handled the implementation of the interactive experience and worked with the team to translate our design ideas into a functional technical concept.

While I focused on the Unity and technical development, the rest of the team worked across the research, design, presentation, and other competition requirements. We collaborated closely to ensure that the technical experience supported our overall goal: making Qasr Amra easier to understand, explore, and appreciate.
