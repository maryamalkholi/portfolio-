Age of the Moon

The official website for the Age of the Moon board game.

UI/UX DesignWeb DevelopmentLive Site

As part of the Age of the Moon board game project, I designed and developed a dedicated website to showcase the game and provide players with an engaging introduction to its world.

I focused on creating a clean, visually appealing UI that matched the game’s identity and atmosphere. The website was designed to make information easy to navigate while maintaining the same visual language as the board game.

This project allowed me to combine UI/UX design and web development, translating the game’s visual identity into an interactive digital experience.
