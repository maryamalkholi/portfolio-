Mapty

Social running — see where your friends are before you start.

JavaScriptGeolocationInteractive Maps

Mapty is a web application designed to make running more social by allowing friends to see each other’s locations and coordinate their runs. The idea was to solve a simple problem: when friends want to go running together, it can be difficult to know where everyone is or whether someone has already started their run.

The website uses location-based data to display users and their positions, allowing runners to better coordinate and meet up. The project gave me practical experience working with APIs, geolocation data, interactive maps, and location-based web functionality.
