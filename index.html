About

Hello, I’m Maryam

Add About2.png

I’m a game designer and developer based in Amman, Jordan, working across gameplay systems, technical design and full-stack web development. Most of what I build starts as a question — what happens if power always costs something, or if the enemy you’re hunting can only be heard and never seen — and turns into a playable system that answers it.

I work mainly in Unity and C#, from the first line of a design document through prototyping, mechanics, balancing and polish. Alongside that I build web applications with JavaScript, Node.js, Python and MongoDB, which keeps me equally comfortable designing a combat loop and shipping a working product.

Outside of my own projects I teach game development to school students, work as a junior designer and developer for an international studio, and volunteer across Jordan’s gaming and tech community. I care most about ideas that are simple to understand and hard to put down.

Mission

I want to design games whose systems say something the story doesn’t have to.

What motivates me is the moment a player understands a mechanic without being told — when a rule they accepted five minutes ago suddenly becomes the reason they’re in trouble. I’m drawn to design where the meaning lives inside the systems: greed that compounds into debt, a machine you built yourself that you might lose, a room whose objects are pieces of someone’s identity.

What I want to contribute is that kind of design thinking to teams building ambitious games, and to the industry growing around me in Jordan and the wider MENA region. I’ve spent the last years learning in public — game jams, hackathons, competitions, teaching classrooms full of students their first line of C# — and I intend to keep opening that door behind me while I build games that are genuinely worth finishing.
